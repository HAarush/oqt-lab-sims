P-N Junction Diode Simulator

1. Aim and Apparatus

Aim: To plot the current-voltage relationship and determine the knee voltage (cut-in voltage) of a P-N junction diode.

Apparatus:

  • P-N junction diode (Silicon).
  • Variable DC power supply (0-3V for forward, 0-30V for reverse).
  • Voltmeter and Milliammeter/Microammeter.
  • Connecting wires and a key.

Expected Results

  • Forward Region: An exponential rise in current after the threshold voltage (~0.7V for Si).
  • Reverse Region: A flat line near zero current followed by a sharp vertical drop at breakdown.

2. Experimental Procedure

A. Forward Bias Setup

In this mode, the diode allows current to flow easily.

  • Connections: Connect the Anode (P-side) to positive and Cathode (N-side) to negative.
  • Measurements: Increase voltage in small steps. Note readings.
  • Observation: Current remains near zero until knee voltage, then increases rapidly.

B. Reverse Bias Setup

In this mode, the diode blocks most current, except tiny leakage.

  • Connections: Connect Anode (P) to negative and Cathode (N) to positive.
  • Measurements: Increase voltage in larger steps. Use microammeter.
  • Observation: Current remains small until breakdown voltage is reached.

Key Precautions

  • Ensure all connections are tight and clean.
  • Do not exceed the breakdown voltage.
  • Always check the polarities before switching power on.
